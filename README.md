## ⏱️ The plan at a glance
- **Tomorrow:** Tell everyone you know, knock 2–3 nearby streets, post in local groups, start your free Google listing. → *1–3 jobs booked.*
- **Next week:** Knock daily near booked jobs, do great work, ask every customer for a review/referral. → *5–10 paying customers.*
- **Next 3 months:** Month 1 foundation (10–20 recurring) → Month 2 density & referrals (40–60) → Month 3 systemise into a steady repeating round.
