# ⚡ Quick Start — Get Your First Customers This Week

**One page. Print it. Do it.** This is the short version of the full plan ([`WINDOW-CLEANING-90-DAY-PLAN.md`](./WINDOW-CLEANING-90-DAY-PLAN.md)). The copy-paste scripts are in the [`templates/`](./templates) folder.

> **The one thing that matters most:** Stop waiting for customers to find you. Go knock on doors near where you live, today. That's how window cleaning rounds get filled. Everything else is a bonus.

---

## ✅ TOMORROW (Day 1) — aim for 1–3 booked jobs

**Morning (1 hr):**
- [ ] Post once on your personal Facebook/Instagram → *"Starting my window cleaning business — first 5 local bookings this week get a discount. Know anyone? Please share!"* (wording: [`templates/facebook-and-nextdoor-posts.md`](./templates/facebook-and-nextdoor-posts.md))
- [ ] Text **10 people you know** locally — ask them and ask them to pass your name on.
- [ ] Start your free **Google Business Profile** (takes a few days to verify, so start now): [`templates/google-business-profile-setup.md`](./templates/google-business-profile-setup.md)

**Afternoon (2–3 hrs) — the money-maker:**
- [ ] Pick **2–3 streets near you** and **knock every door** using the script: [`templates/door-knock-script.md`](./templates/door-knock-script.md)
- [ ] No answer? Leave a **"Sorry I missed you" card** with your number: [`templates/flyer-text.md`](./templates/flyer-text.md)
- [ ] Write down every yes/maybe (address + price) in your tracker.

**Evening (30 min):**
- [ ] Post in **2–3 local Facebook groups / Nextdoor** ("[Town] Community / Recommendations"): [`templates/facebook-and-nextdoor-posts.md`](./templates/facebook-and-nextdoor-posts.md)

---

## ✅ THIS WEEK (Days 2–7) — aim for 5–10 paying customers

**Every day:**
- [ ] **Knock doors 2+ hours**, focusing on streets **next to a job you've already booked** (neighbours say yes more).
- [ ] Do your booked jobs **brilliantly**; text the customer after: *"Cleaned your windows today, £X, thanks — see you in 4 weeks!"*
- [ ] After every happy job, **ask for a review + a referral** (offer a small reward): [`templates/review-request-message.md`](./templates/review-request-message.md)

**Once this week:**
- [ ] Finish Google Profile + add **before/after photos**.
- [ ] Make a simple free **Facebook business Page**.
- [ ] Order cheap **business cards + flyers + door hangers**: [`templates/flyer-text.md`](./templates/flyer-text.md)
- [ ] Sort out an **easy way to get paid** (bank transfer/cash/app).
- [ ] Sort your **pricing** and start a **customer/round tracker**: [`templates/pricing-and-quote-sheet.md`](./templates/pricing-and-quote-sheet.md)

## 🎯 Targets
| When | Conversations / quotes | Customers |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | 5–10 | 1–3 booked |
| **End of Week 1** | 10–20 | 5–10 paying |
| **End of Month 1** | 40–80 | 10–20 recurring |

## 🔑 Remember
1. **Door-knocking near existing customers** is your #1 tool. Do it daily.
2. **Recurring customers** (cleaned every 4 weeks) are the goal — not one-offs.
3. **Be reliable.** Just turning up every time beats most of your competition.
4. **Always ask** for reviews and referrals at the happy moment.
