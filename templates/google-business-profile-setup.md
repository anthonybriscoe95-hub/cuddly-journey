# 🔎 Google Business Profile — Get Found on Google & Maps (free)

When someone searches **"window cleaner near me"**, Google shows a map with local businesses. A **Google Business Profile** (free) is how you appear there. It's one of the best free customer sources — set it up Day 1 because **verification can take a few days**.

---

## Why it matters
- It's **free** and puts you on Google Search + Google Maps.
- Customers can find your phone number, see your reviews, and call you directly.
- **Reviews here are gold** — they're the first thing people check. Every happy customer = ask for a Google review.

---

## Step-by-step setup
1. Go to **google.com/business** (or search "Google Business Profile") and sign in with a Google account (make a free one if needed).
2. **Enter your business name** (e.g. "[Your Name] Window Cleaning") and choose the category **"Window cleaning service"**.
3. **Service-area business:** since you go to customers, choose **"I deliver goods and services to my customers"** and **hide your home address** — instead set your **service areas** (your town + nearby areas/postcodes). You don't need a shopfront.
4. **Add your phone number** and (optionally) a website — a free Facebook Page link works if you don't have a site.
5. **Set your service areas** to every town/area you'll cover.
6. **Verify** — Google confirms you're real (usually by postcard, phone, email, or video; method varies). **Do this promptly** — your profile won't show publicly until verified, and the postcard option can take several days.
7. While waiting, **fill everything in:** hours, services offered (residential, commercial, conservatory, one-off), a short description, and your service areas.

---

## After it's live — make it work
- **Add photos:** your van, you working, and **before/after** shots of real jobs. Profiles with photos get far more clicks. Add new ones regularly.
- **Write a short description**, e.g.:
  > "[Business Name] provides reliable, friendly window cleaning for homes and businesses across [Town] and surrounding areas. Regular 4-weekly cleans, frames and sills included, fully insured. Free quotes — call [phone]."
- **Turn on messaging** so people can contact you easily.
- **Get reviews relentlessly:** after every happy clean, send your review link (your profile gives you a short link to share). Aim for a steady stream — **the more recent reviews, the higher you rank.**
- **Reply to every review** (a quick "Thanks [Name], see you next time!"). It looks great and helps ranking.
- **Post updates** occasionally (offers, "taking on new customers in [area]") — these show on your profile.

---

## Also worth doing (free local listings)
- **Bing Places** (same idea as Google, takes 5 minutes).
- Free local directories / trade-recommendation sites popular in your country.
- Your **Facebook business Page** (links nicely from here).

> 💡 Don't wait for Google to bring customers before you knock doors — it builds slowly over weeks. Treat it as a long-term tap that's filling in the background while door-knocking gets you customers **today**.
