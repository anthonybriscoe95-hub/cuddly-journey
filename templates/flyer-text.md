# 🪧 Flyers, Business Cards & Door Hangers (copy-paste wording)

Cheap to print online. The **"Sorry I missed you" door hanger is the most valuable one** — it turns every no-answer door into a possible callback. Replace the [brackets] with your details before printing.

## Printing & using them — quick tips
- **Online print shops** (e.g. Vistaprint and similar) print cards/flyers very cheaply — you don't need many to start.
- Put your **phone number BIG** — it's the only thing that matters on the page.
- **Door hangers > letterbox flyers** for no-answer houses (they don't get lost in the post).
- Keep cards on you **always** — hand one to every person you talk to.
- A **simple logo** isn't essential to start; clear text + a phone number beats a fancy design with no number.
- Add a small **discount/offer** ("10% off first clean") — it gives people a reason to call now.
