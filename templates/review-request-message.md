# ⭐ Asking for Reviews & Referrals (your free growth engine)

Every happy customer can bring you **2–3 more** through reviews and referrals — for free. The secret is simple: **always ask, and ask at the happy moment** (right after a great clean, when they're pleased). Most people are glad to help; they just need to be asked.

---

## When to ask
- **Right after a great first clean** — they're seeing sparkling windows, this is the peak moment.
- In your **"I've cleaned today" text** (easy, no pressure).
- After they say something nice ("Oh that looks great!") → "Thank you! Would you mind leaving me a quick review? It really helps."

---

## 1. Asking for a Google review (text/message)
> Hi [Name], thanks so much for having me clean your windows today! 😊 If you were happy with the job, would you mind leaving me a quick **Google review**? I'm a small local business and it genuinely makes a huge difference. Here's the link — takes 30 seconds: [your review link] 🙏 Thank you!

**In person:**
> "Really glad you're happy with it! If you get a sec, a quick Google review would honestly mean a lot to a small business like mine — I can text you the link."

---

## 2. Asking for referrals (with a reward)
> By the way [Name] — if any of your neighbours or friends would like their windows done too, please send them my way! I'll give you **[£5 off your next clean / a free clean]** for anyone who signs up. 🙂 Thanks so much!

**Why a reward works:** it gives the customer a reason to actually mention you, and neighbours are the easiest, cheapest customers you'll ever get (less driving, easy "yes").

---

## 3. The neighbour knock (referral in action)
After signing someone up, knock next door:
> "Hi! I've just started cleaning [neighbour]'s windows at number [X] on a regular round — would you like yours done too while I'm on the street?"

People say yes far more when they know a neighbour already uses you.

---

## 4. Follow-up if they forget the review
A gentle one-time nudge a few days later:
> Hi [Name], hope your windows are still looking great! ✨ No worries if you're busy, but if you did have a moment for that quick Google review I'd really appreciate it — here's the link again: [link]. Thanks so much! 🙏

---

## How to get your Google review link
Once your **Google Business Profile** is verified, it gives you a short **"Ask for reviews"** link you can copy and text/email to customers. Save it in your phone so it's one tap to send. (Setup: [`google-business-profile-setup.md`](./google-business-profile-setup.md).)

---

## Make it a habit (this is the whole trick)
- **Every. Single. Happy. Customer.** Ask for a review **and** mention the referral reward.
- Keep your review link saved for instant sending.
- **Reply to every review** you get ("Thanks [Name], see you next clean!").
- Reviews compound: more reviews → higher on Google → more customers → more reviews. Start now, even with one customer.
