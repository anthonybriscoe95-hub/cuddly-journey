# 🚪 Door-Knocking Script (your #1 customer-getter)

Door-knocking ("canvassing") is the fastest, cheapest, most reliable way to fill a window cleaning round. Most people are friendly. You'll get plenty of "no thanks" — that's normal, just move to the next door. **A few yeses a day = a full round in a few months.**

## Mindset before you start
- **It's a numbers game.** Knock ~30–50 doors and you'll typically pick up a few customers. Every "no" gets you closer to a "yes."
- **Smile, be brief, be normal.** You're offering a useful local service, not begging.
- **Knock streets near where you live or near a customer you already have** (density = less driving, easier yeses).
- **Best times:** late afternoon / early evening and weekends, when people are home. Avoid meal times.
- Wear something tidy with your business name if you have it. Carry cards.

---

## The basic script (residential)
> **"Hi there! I'm [Name], I'm a local window cleaner just starting to take on a few houses on this street. Would you be interested in having your windows cleaned regularly?"**

Then pause and let them answer. That's it. Keep it short and friendly.

**If they say "How much?"**
> "For a house like yours it'd be about **£[X]**, and I'd come back every **4 weeks** to keep them looking great. The first clean's a little more as it's always the dirtiest one, then it's £[X] each time after."

**If they say "Yes / maybe":**
> "Brilliant! Can I grab your name and number, and I'll [do it now / pop back on date]? Here's my card."
> → **Write the address, name, number, and price in your tracker immediately.**

**If they say "No thanks":**
> "No problem at all — here's my card in case you ever change your mind. Have a great day!"
> → Smile, leave a card, move on. Don't take it personally.

---

## Handling the common objections
**"I already have a window cleaner."**
> "Ah, no worries! If they ever let you down or stop showing up, give me a call — I'm reliable and I'm local. Here's my card." *(Lots of cleaners quietly disappear — you'll get these customers later.)*

**"I do them myself."**
> "Fair enough! A lot of my customers used to as well — it's just nice not to climb ladders. Keep my card in case you fancy handing it over one day. Cheers!"

**"Not right now / I'll think about it."**
> "Of course. Here's my card — and if it helps, I'm doing a few of your neighbours on [day], so I'll be on the street anyway. Just text me if you'd like to join."

**"How do I know you'll keep coming?"**
> "Great question — that's actually my whole thing. I clean [this street/area] on a 4-weekly round, I text you each time I've been, and you can stop any time. I'm building a regular round right here, so you'll see me about."

**"Can you do the inside too / conservatory / gutters?"**
> "Yes — exterior is the regular every-4-weeks clean; insides/conservatory/extra bits I can quote separately. Want me to take a look?"

---

## Commercial version (shops, cafés, offices — great for Month 2+)
Walk in, ask for the manager/owner, and keep it quick:
> **"Hi, I'm [Name], a local window cleaner. I look after a few businesses nearby and I'm offering regular shopfront cleans — weekly or fortnightly — to keep your windows spotless for customers. Could I give you a quick price?"**

- Emphasise **regular** (weekly/fortnightly) and **clustered** — "I'm already doing [shop down the road]."
- Mention you're **insured** (have public liability ready — many businesses require it).
- Leave a card and a one-line price. Follow up in a week.

---

## After a "yes" — lock it in
1. Save the customer in your **round tracker** (name, address, phone, price, next date).
2. **Knock the houses either side and opposite** — *"I'm just signing up [neighbour] at number X, would you like yours done too?"* This is the easiest yes you'll get.
3. Do a great first clean, **text them after**, and **ask for a review + referral**.

> 💡 **The compounding trick:** every new customer is a reason to knock their neighbours. Work the street, not the town.
