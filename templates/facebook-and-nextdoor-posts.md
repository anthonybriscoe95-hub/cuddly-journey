## Posting tips
- **Join every local group** you can: "[Town] Community", "[Town] Recommendations", "[Town] Buy/Sell", village/estate groups, "[Town] Small Businesses".
- **Post photos** — a crisp before/after of a real job gets far more attention than text alone.
- **Reply fast** to anyone asking for a cleaner. Speed wins these.
- **Don't spam** — one good post per group, then engage genuinely. Re-post every couple of weeks if rules allow.
- **Always answer publicly first** ("Sent you a message!") so the whole group sees you're active and responsive.
